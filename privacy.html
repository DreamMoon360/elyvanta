Privacy Policy

Elyvanta AI Studio · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who uses this app

Elyvanta AI Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to create videos for, and manage, the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to:

  • upload videos and Shorts to the owner's YouTube channel (as private videos for review);
  • set video thumbnails, upload captions and add videos to playlists;
  • read the channel's own videos and YouTube Analytics statistics.

How the data is used and stored

This data is used only to perform the actions above for the owner's channel. The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer. Channel data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Elyvanta AI Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
